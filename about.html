About Me

My professional journey has focused on building and operating reliable, secure and scalable technology platforms.

As a DevOps / Platform Engineering Lead, I work across Kubernetes infrastructure, cloud-native architecture, automation, deployment governance, observability and platform security.

My experience includes:

• Managing enterprise Kubernetes environments and containerized workloads.

• Implementing GitOps-based application delivery using Argo CD and Helm.

• Improving platform reliability through high availability and disaster recovery practices.

• Strengthening platform security through RBAC, OIDC, Vault, Keycloak and vulnerability management.

• Building monitoring and observability capabilities using Prometheus, Grafana and ELK.

• Exploring AIOps and AI-assisted operational automation.

• Coordinating technical delivery and collaborating with distributed engineering teams.

I have also contributed to international projects involving Valmet, Dassault Systèmes and Ericsson across Finland and Sweden.

I enjoy solving complex engineering challenges, mentoring technical teams and building platforms that make software delivery more reliable and efficient.
