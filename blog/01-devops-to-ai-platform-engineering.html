From DevOps to AI Platform Engineering

How established DevOps, Kubernetes, security and observability practices provide the foundation for AI-enabled platforms.

Publication note: This article is an editable starter draft. Verify examples, replace illustrative material with sanitised evidence, and label prototype vs production work accurately before publishing.

A practical perspective on how platform engineering foundations can support secure, reliable AI-enabled systems.

Why platform engineering is a foundation for AI

AI services still depend on reliable infrastructure, delivery pipelines, identity, observability, security controls and operational processes. Kubernetes, GitOps and automation provide useful building blocks for running these services consistently.

What carries forward from DevOps

  • Automated delivery and versioned configuration
  • Observability, incident response and service reliability
  • Security controls, access boundaries and secrets management
  • Infrastructure automation and reproducible environments

What AI platforms add

  • Model serving and model lifecycle concerns
  • Prompt and retrieval management
  • Evaluation, tracing and quality monitoring
  • Vector search and data governance

My learning and implementation path

I am building practical experiments around local LLMs, RAG, agent workflows and operational evidence. These are learning/prototype activities unless explicitly identified as production implementation.

Conclusion

AI Platform Engineering is an extension of platform engineering: reliable foundations, safe interfaces and measurable operations remain essential.
