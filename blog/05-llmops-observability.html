LLMOps: Observability, Evaluation and Governance

Treat AI workflows as production services with traces, evaluation, reliability metrics, privacy and governance.

Publication note: This article is an editable starter draft. Verify examples, replace illustrative material with sanitised evidence, and label prototype vs production work accurately before publishing.

AI workflows need operational monitoring and quality evaluation alongside conventional service observability.

Trace the workflow

Record the stages of intake, retrieval, model calls, tool execution and final response. Protect traces from sensitive data leakage.

Evaluate quality

  • Retrieval relevance and source coverage
  • Groundedness and factual correctness
  • Tool selection and execution success
  • Latency, token usage and cost
  • Human acceptance and failure categories

Governance

Define data retention, access controls, model/version tracking, evaluation gates and approval boundaries before expanding usage.

Operational readiness

Alert on service-level symptoms, establish fallback behaviour and review changes to prompts, retrieval and models through a controlled release process.
