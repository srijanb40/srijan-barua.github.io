Hybrid RAG for Operational Knowledge

Combining semantic and keyword search to retrieve relevant operational knowledge and ground generated responses.

Publication note: This article is an editable starter draft. Verify examples, replace illustrative material with sanitised evidence, and label prototype vs production work accurately before publishing.

Hybrid retrieval can improve the grounding of operational assistants by combining lexical matching with semantic similarity.

Why retrieval matters

LLMs do not inherently know the latest internal runbooks or incident records. Retrieval supplies relevant source material at response time.

Keyword and vector search

Keyword search is useful for exact error strings, IDs and product names. Semantic search helps retrieve conceptually similar incidents and documentation. A hybrid strategy can combine their strengths.

Suggested pipeline

  1. Ingest and clean approved documents.
  2. Chunk content with metadata.
  3. Generate embeddings.
  4. Run lexical and vector retrieval.
  5. Rank, deduplicate and filter results.
  6. Provide source references to the model.

Quality checks

Measure recall, precision, relevance, source freshness and whether generated answers are supported by retrieved evidence.
