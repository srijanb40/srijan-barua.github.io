AI-Assisted Kubernetes Security and Anomaly Detection

A proposed architecture for correlating vulnerability, deployment, metric and log signals to support prioritised investigation.

Publication note: This article is an editable starter draft. Verify examples, replace illustrative material with sanitised evidence, and label prototype vs production work accurately before publishing.

A reference design for combining Kubernetes security signals and observability data to assist prioritisation and investigation.

Signals

Potential inputs include image scan findings, deployment changes, Kubernetes events, metrics and logs. The sources should be authorised and sanitised.

Correlation and prioritisation

Combine severity with workload context, exposure, recent change information and available evidence. AI may help summarise relationships, but deterministic policy should govern risk thresholds.

Response workflow

  1. Collect and normalise signals.
  2. Correlate workload and deployment metadata.
  3. Identify suspicious patterns or unusual behaviour.
  4. Produce evidence and recommended checks.
  5. Route to the responsible engineer.

Guardrails and limitations

Do not allow the model to execute remediation directly. Validate alerts, account for false positives, protect sensitive data and document that this is a proposed or prototype architecture until deployed and evaluated.
